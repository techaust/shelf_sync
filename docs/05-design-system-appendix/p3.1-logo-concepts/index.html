ShelfSync logo concepts

Three directions for the ShelfSync symbol, drawn on the TecHaust mark's construction: solid blocks on a 100-unit grid, one 45° channel exactly one stroke wide, ink with a verdigris accent. Pick one; P3.2 refines and audits it.

The wordmark is Archivo at weight 620 and 116 % width, outlined from the font with its own kerning. Bespoke letter edits come in P3.2.

1 Shelves

An ink upright carries two shelves. The verdigris product is still sliding in on the middle shelf: its leading edge is cut at 45°, one stroke clear of the ink.

concept-1-shelves.svg

At small size

Strongest: the open bracket and the proud verdigris bar keep their shape at 16 px, and the mark looks like nothing else in the tray.

Weakest: it reads first as a capital E (close to a euro sign at tray size), and the ink stub ahead of the channel is only 2 to 5 px wide at 16 px, so the cut reads as a small notch.

Rule checks

  • Pass

    Solid blocks, 100-unit grid. Every edge is on the grid except the cut ends (45.25, 72.12), which follow from a one-stroke channel at 45°, as TecHaust's 73.13 does.

  • Pass

    One decorative device. One channel, in the middle bar. No other cut, curve or detail.

  • Pass

    Channel exactly one stroke. 19 units square to the cut (26.87 across), equal to the vertical stroke. At 16 px it is 4 px across, 2.8 px square to the cut, as in TecHaust's favicon.

  • Pass

    Horizontals about 8 % thinner. 17.5 against 19 (7.9 %) in the vector and 11 against 12 px at 64 px. At 32 and 16 px they are equal, the same pixel-fitting rule as TecHaust's.

  • Pass

    Ink base, verdigris accent. Verdigris only on the product bar, the smallest share of the three concepts. 6.3 to 1 on white.

  • Pass

    Colour on dark. Mist with verdigris-300: 13.4 and 7.9 to 1 on the ink-900 app bar, 13.5 and 7.9 to 1 on the dark taskbar mock.

  • Pass

    No sync clichés. No arrows, circles, clouds or ticks. The upright keeps the three bars from reading as a menu icon.

2 S-cut

An S from two offset blocks: the ink top half and the verdigris bottom half meet in the spine, split by the one-stroke channel, as the T and H share a channel in TecHaust's mark.

concept-2-s-cut.svg

At small size

Strongest: the closest relative of the TecHaust mark, and the letter gives the name; the two halves stay apart at every size.

Weakest: a square S built from blocks can be taken for a 5, and in dark mist and verdigris-300 differ more in hue than in lightness (1.7 to 1), so the split between the halves leans on the 4 px gap.

Rule checks

  • Pass

    Solid blocks, 100-unit grid. Every edge is on the grid except the cut ends (45.32, 72.19), which follow from a one-stroke channel at 45°, as TecHaust's 73.13 does.

  • Pass

    One decorative device. One channel, in the spine. The terminals are square.

  • Pass

    Channel exactly one stroke. 19 units square to the cut (26.87 across), equal to the vertical stroke. At 16 px it is 4 px across, 2.8 px square to the cut, as in TecHaust's favicon.

  • Pass

    Horizontals about 8 % thinner. 17.5 against 19 (7.9 %) in the vector and 11 against 12 px at 64 px. At 32 and 16 px they are equal, the same pixel-fitting rule as TecHaust's.

  • Note

    Ink base, verdigris accent. Never swapped, but verdigris takes half the mark, more than the other two concepts and more than "used sparingly" suggests. 6.3 to 1 on white.

  • Pass

    Colour on dark. Mist with verdigris-300: 13.4 and 7.9 to 1 on the ink-900 app bar, 13.5 and 7.9 to 1 on the dark taskbar mock.

  • Pass

    No sync clichés. No arrows, circles, clouds or ticks. The channel runs across the spine, so there is no arrowhead.

3 One to many

One ink block, the file, feeds two verdigris stems, the stores. A single 45° channel separates them; its far edge cuts the tops of both stems.

concept-3-one-to-many.svg

At small size

Strongest: one clean 45° line runs through the bar end and both stem tops, and it still reads as one line at 16 px; the idea of one file reaching every store is the most literal of the three.

Weakest: at 16 px the two verdigris stems read as a rising bar chart, a stock stats icon; the middle stem's tip sits under open space, so the feeding needs explaining; and the top right corner is TecHaust's own construction.

Rule checks

  • Pass

    Solid blocks, 100-unit grid. Every edge is on the grid except the cut ends (73.13), which follow from a one-stroke channel at 45°, as TecHaust's 73.13 does.

  • Note

    One decorative device. One channel, but it cuts two stem tops along its far edge: one line, two cut ends. P3.2 should rule whether that counts as one device.

  • Pass

    Channel exactly one stroke. 19 units square to the cut (26.87 across), equal to the vertical stroke. At 16 px it is 4 px across, 2.8 px square to the cut, as in TecHaust's favicon.

  • Pass

    Horizontals about 8 % thinner. 17.5 against 19 (7.9 %) in the vector and 11 against 12 px at 64 px. At 32 and 16 px they are equal, the same pixel-fitting rule as TecHaust's.

  • Pass

    Ink base, verdigris accent. Ink carries the bar and the first stem; verdigris the two stores. 6.3 to 1 on white.

  • Pass

    Colour on dark. Mist with verdigris-300: 13.4 and 7.9 to 1 on the ink-900 app bar, 13.5 and 7.9 to 1 on the dark taskbar mock.

  • Note

    No sync clichés. No arrows, circles, clouds or ticks. Not a banned motif, but the two stepped verdigris stems recall a bar chart or signal-strength icon at small sizes.

Colours: the Patina palette from TecHaust's packages/ui/tokens/tokens.json, set once at the top of this page. The two taskbar greys are mixed from the white and black tokens to stand in for Windows 11; the real taskbar is checked when the tray icons are built (P3.3).

Sample content: the taskbar clock and the neighbouring tray icons are placeholders.
