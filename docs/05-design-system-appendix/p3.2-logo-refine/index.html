ShelfSync logo, refined

P3.2 refines concept 2, S-cut. Verdigris becomes the accent, the S stops reading as a 5, the split holds on dark at 16 px, every size is pixel-fitted, and the wordmark takes the f-crossbar cut. Every number and shape on this page is generated from brand/draft/geometry.mjs.

3 Not a 5

A square S drawn from blocks is the same figure as a square 5. The fix stays inside the construction: the top terminal turns down and the bottom terminal turns up, by a spur of 9 units (round(0.09 × size) px, at least 2 px so it reads as a turn and not as a thicker bar end). A 5 never turns its top bar down, and the turned ends make the S point-symmetric. At 16 px that needs room: see the pixel-fitting rule in step 5. Shown in one colour, so only the shape is compared.

4 The split on dark at 16 px

Mist against verdigris-300 is 1.71 : 1 (lightness L* 92.4 against 73.4; they differ mainly in hue, ΔE2000 22.9). So the split must not depend on colour. With variant A, the top and lower blocks are both mist on dark and are separated only by the channel, which at 16 px leaves 3 fully clear background pixels on every spine row (coverage at most 2 %), edged at 13.44 : 1 and 7.86 : 1 against the ink-900 bar. Both cut edges run through pixel corners, so each edge pixel is exactly half covered and none of them bridges the gap. Verdigris touches mist only along a 3 px joint inside the lower block, which the S does not need in order to read.

5 Pixel fitting

TecHaust's pixelMark rule, applied to the S: vertical = round(0.19 × size), at least 2; below 40 px horizontals equal verticals, above it round(0.175 × size); the channel's run is round(vertical × √2), so both cut ends land on whole pixels and the cut stays exactly 45°. The spine top is round(0.485 × size − horizontal / 2). One difference from TecHaust, at 16 px only: the S stacks three horizontals in 16 pixels where TecHaust's TH stacks two, so with 3 px horizontals a 2 px spur would leave a 1 px opening, which closes up at true size. The rule is: if the opening between spur and spine would fall under 2 px, the horizontals lose 1 px. That happens only at 16 px (horizontals 2, verticals 3); at 20, 24, 32, 48 and 64 px TecHaust's rule holds unchanged. The owner's screen runs at 125 %, so each raster is shown enlarged (with its pixel grid up to 32 px) and once at true device size.

6 Wordmark

Archivo at weight 620 and width 116 %, tracking -6 font units, outlined with HarfBuzz. The f crossbar's right end is cut "/" across its whole 15.43-unit height, the construction of TecHaust's T bar. Spacing was measured with TecHaust's method (white area per pair against the font's own "no") and five pairs were tightened.

Before (P3.1)
After

How the pairs were set. Sh, lf and yn were 8.5, 8.2 and 9.0 % loose and now sit within 3.3 % of "no" (computed before rounding; from the table's rounded values it is 3.4 %). fS and Sy are open by nature (60 and 59 % of their scanlines hit the cap, like TecHaust's Te), so they were tightened in whole units only while their closest approach stayed at or above 7.8 units, the tightest in-rhythm pair (yn). They stopped at 8.5 and 7.9; one more unit would take them to 7.5 and 6.9, under 7.8.

The method was checked first on TecHaust's own wordmark: it reproduces the published values within 0.4 units (table below).

Rule checks

Every rule in 05 §1.2–1.3 and every owner decision for P3.2.

  • Pass

    Solid blocks on a 100-unit grid. Every edge is on the grid except the two cut ends (45.31 and 72.18), which follow from a one-stroke channel at 45°, as TecHaust's 73.13 does.

  • Pass

    One decorative device: the 45° cut, one stroke wide. One channel, 19 square to the cut at every vector size; 94.3 %, 106.1 %, 99.0 %, 94.3 %, 102.1 %, 100.2 % of the stroke at 16, 20, 24, 32, 48, 64 px (the build checks the vector channel equals the stroke). The spurs are square letter terminals, not a second device.

  • Note

    Horizontals about 8 % thinner. 17.5 against 19 (7.9 %). Pixel-fitted: 11/12 at 64 px, 8/9 at 48 px, equal at 32 px (TecHaust's rule), and 2/3 at 16 px, where the S needs the room for its spurs (step 5). The build checks 7 to 9 % in the vector and thinner horizontals at 40 px and above.

  • Pass

    Ink base, verdigris accent, never swapped. Verdigris is 10.6 % of the inked area (was 50.5 %).

  • Pass

    On dark: mist and verdigris-300. 13.44 and 7.86 : 1 on ink-900.

  • Pass

    Works at 16 px, pixel-fitted at 64 px and below. Every coordinate is a whole pixel at 16, 20, 24, 32, 48, 64 px, both cuts are 45° "/", and every spur and opening is at least 2 px; the build checks all of it.

  • Pass

    No arrows, circles of arrows, clouds or tick boxes. None. The channel crosses the spine, so it never makes an arrowhead.

  • Pass

    Wordmark: Archivo 620 / 116 %, outlined, f-crossbar cut. Outlined with HarfBuzz; the f crossbar is cut "/" at 45° across its full 15.43-unit height (checked by the build); tracking and five pair kerns are numbers in the geometry.

  • Pass

    Endorsement in IBM Plex Mono caps at caption size. Outlined; it reaches 12 px (caption size) when the endorsed lockup is 266 px wide, so that is its minimum.

  • Pass

    Clear space of one stroke. 24.32 units at lockup scale (19 × 1.28), shown below.

  • Misses

    Minimum sizes: primary 96 px wide, symbol 16 px. At 96 px wide the primary's symbol is only 12.94 px and its cap height 10.11 px, under the symbol's own 16 px minimum. The primary needs 119 px for a 16 px symbol (suggested change to 05, not made).

  • Pass

    Colour versions: on light, on dark, one colour. All three are drawn for all four lockups; the white and black one-colour files come with the asset set (P3.3).

  • Pass

    Owner: concept 2, S-cut, is the logo. Refined, not redrawn: same blocks, channel and proportions as P3.1.

  • Pass

    Owner: verdigris clearly the smaller part. 10.6 % (variant A, recommended); the alternatives are 18.6 % and 26.4 %.

  • Pass

    Owner: the f crossbar carries the 45° cut, one stroke wide. The cut runs across the whole bar (15.43 units), the same construction as TecHaust's T bar.

  • Pass

    Owner: the cut always runs "/". The build checks both channel edges at every pixel size, and the f cut's direction ("/"), angle (45°) and height (the full 15.43-unit bar). The vector channel edges are drawn from the same frame and are checked visually on the plate.
