**Roadmap:** P3.1 ([08](../08-roadmap.md)). Exit: the owner picks one concept (lead at Opus 5.5 high for that choice, [12 §3](../12-models-and-usage.md)).

Stop it at the end.

**Rules (all from [05 §1.2–1.3](../05-design-system.md), read them in full):**
- Construction: solid blocks on a 100-unit grid; one decorative device, the 45° diagonal cut, exactly one stroke wide; horizontals about 8 % thinner than verticals.
- Colour: ink base + verdigris accent, never swapped; on dark, mist + light verdigris. `brand/tokens.json` doesn't exist yet (P3.3), so take the Patina values from TecHaust's `packages/ui/tokens/tokens.json` in `techaust_platform` (read-only) and put them once as CSS custom properties at the top of the board, each with a comment naming its token. No other hand-typed colours.
- Must read at 16 px. No arrows, arrow circles, clouds or boxes with a tick.
- Reference for the construction language: the TecHaust mark and its docs in `techaust_platform` (read-only; never change it; never read any `CREDENTIALS` folder).
- Wordmark: "ShelfSync" in Archivo, weight 620, width 116 %. For concepts, live text from a locally available copy of Archivo is fine if outlining isn't practical yet; say which you did. If Archivo isn't available locally, stop and report (no downloads, no new packages).

**The three concepts** (starting directions from 05 §1.2; refine freely within the rules, but keep each idea recognisable):
1. **Shelves:** three stacked shelf bars, the middle one verdigris carrying the diagonal cut.
2. **S-cut:** an "S" from two offset blocks separated by the one-stroke diagonal channel (the TecHaust TH-ligature construction).
3. **One to many:** one ink block feeding two verdigris blocks through the diagonal channel.

**Deliverables** in `docs/05-design-system-appendix/p3.1-logo-concepts/`:
- `concept-1-shelves.svg`, `concept-2-s-cut.svg`, `concept-3-one-to-many.svg`: the symbol on a 100-unit viewBox, ink + verdigris.
- `index.html`: one comparison board. For each concept: the symbol at 16, 32 and 64 px (pixel-fitted at 64 px and below), the primary horizontal lockup on light and on dark, and the tray icon at 16 px on a mock Windows 11 taskbar (light and dark). A short line per concept: the idea and its strongest/weakest point at small size. It must work in light and dark.
- Screenshots of the board (light and dark, desktop width) saved next to it as `board-light.png` / `board-dark.png` if the browser tools can save them; if they only return images to you, say so and describe them.

**Done means:** the three SVGs and the board exist; every rule above is checked and stated per concept with an honest note (pass, or what misses); your own screenshots viewed at 16 px for each symbol; §4 filled in.

**Out of scope:** refining and auditing (P3.2), `brand/` and the generator (P3.3), mockups (P3.4), any change to `docs/05-design-system.md` (report suggestions instead).

**Construction** (TecHaust values): vertical 19, horizontal 17.5, crossbar centre 48.5, channel 19 measured square to a 45° "/" cut (26.87 across). Pixel fitting follows TecHaust's `pixelMark`: v = round(0.19·px), h = v below 40 px, channel = round(v·√2).

**Rule checks**
| Rule | 1 Shelves | 2 S-cut | 3 One to many |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blocks on 100-unit grid | Pass (cut ends 45.25 / 72.12) | Pass (45.32 / 72.19) | Pass (73.13) |

**At 16 px** (judged from the 6× canvas view of the real 16 px raster): Shelves reads first as a capital E (close to €), and the ink stub before the channel is only 2–5 px. S-cut reads clearly as an S. It could be taken for a 5, and in dark mode the split between the halves depends on the 4 px gap (mist against verdigris-300 is 1.7:1). One to many has the clearest diagonal, but the two verdigris stems read as a rising bar chart.

**Wordmark:** outlined, not live text. It is "ShelfSync" in Archivo at wght 620 and wdth 116, with cap height 100, the font's own kerning and −6 tracking, shaped with the HarfBuzz and fontverter copies already in `techaust_platform`'s `node_modules`. They were only read; nothing was installed or written there. There are no bespoke letter edits yet (no cut on the S-bar or the f-crossbar).

**Commands:** a node generator in the session scratchpad (`gen/outline.mjs`, `gen/geometry.mjs`, `gen/build.mjs`), run with `node build.mjs <folder>`. It is not in the project, and P3.3 rebuilds it in `brand/`. A contrast script gave the ratios above. A PowerShell System.Drawing script stitched the screenshot tiles. The preview server `design-appendix` was started, then stopped.
