## 4. Designer report
_(by the designer, Opus 5.5, 2026-10-26)_

**Skills loaded:** `frontend-design` first. It shaped the board as one left-aligned engineering document: the construction plate is the hero, numbers sit next to every figure, and step numbers are used only because the steps are a real sequence. It also ruled out card kits, all-caps eyebrows and mono labels (mono is used only for file names). `dataviz` was loaded for the spacing chart. The chart is a dot plot (before ring, after dot) against a reference band, with text tokens only, a legend, native hover titles and a full table view. The band got visible edges in `border-control` so it passes 3:1 non-text contrast.

**Recommendation: variant A, "cut piece"** (verdigris is only the spine end that the channel cuts off). Its verdigris share is **10.6 %** (vector) and **9.3 %** (16 px raster). B "lower bowl" is 18.6 % / 20.9 %, C "lower terminal" is 26.4 % / 24.6 %, and P3.1 was 50.5 %. Reasons for A:
1. It is the only split where the accent is exactly what the cut produces. The one device and the one accent coincide, which is TecHaust's own grammar.
2. It is clearly an accent.
3. On dark, nothing about the S's shape depends on the colour.

Its weak point: at 16 px the accent is a 2-row flick of 5 to 7 px.

**Key measurements** (all generated, also in `measurements.json`):
- **Construction:** v 19, h 17.5 (7.9 % thinner), channel 19 measured square to the cut (run 26.87), spine centre 48.5, cut ends 45.31 / 72.18, new spur 9 (47.4 % of a stroke).
- **Pixel fit** (v / h / spur / channel run / channel ⟂ as % of stroke / openings):
  - 16 px: 3 / 2 / 2 / 4 / 94.3 % / 3 and 3
  - 32 px: 6 / 6 / 3 / 8 / 94.3 % / 4 and 4

  - 24 px (tray at 150 %): 5 / 5 / 2 / 7 / 99.0 % / 2 and 3

  At all six sizes all 26 coordinates are whole pixels and both cuts are 45° "/" (the build checks this).
- **Not a 5:** at 16 px, a square 5 drawn with the same strokes differs from P3.1's S in 12 px (only the channel). It differs from P3.2's S in 20 px (the channel plus two 3 × 2 spurs). At 32 px it is 48 → 84 px.
- **Dark split at 16 px:** every spine row has 3 fully clear background pixels (coverage ≤ 2 %), edged at 13.44 : 1 (mist) and 7.86 : 1 (verdigris-300) on ink-900. Verdigris meets mist only along a 3-pixel joint. The halves are 1.71 : 1 on dark (L* 92.4 against 73.4, ΔE2000 22.9) and 2.57 : 1 on light (ΔE2000 24.6). How the split holds: both blocks are mist on dark and are separated only by the pixel-fitted channel, so colour carries nothing structural. The board shows the same raster with the verdigris removed to prove it.
- **Contrast:**
  - Ink on white 16.28, verdigris on white 6.34, on stone-50 15.15 / 5.90.
  - Mist on ink-900 13.44, verdigris-300 on ink-900 7.86, on ink-950 15.19 / 8.89.
  - Taskbar mock: light 14.54 / 5.66, dark 13.45 / 7.87.
  - One colour: 16.28.
- **Wordmark white area** (TecHaust's method reproduced: scanlines baseline to x-height, capped at 27.5. On TecHaust's own wordmark it gives no 20.7 / cH 24.0 / Ha 23.3 / st 22.9 against the published 20.3 / 24.1 / 23.2 / 23.1, max difference 0.4). Reference "no" = 20.7. They were tightened only while their closest approach stayed at or above 7.8 (yn, the tightest in-rhythm pair): fS ends at 8.5 and Sy at 7.9 (before: 11.1 and 10.9); one more unit would take them to 7.5 and 6.9. Tracking stays at −6. The f-crossbar cut runs from (391.17, 23.3) to (375.74, 38.73): 15.43 across and 15.43 down, "/". The wordmark is now 772.89 units wide (was 784.89); the primary lockup is 949.53 × 143 units.
